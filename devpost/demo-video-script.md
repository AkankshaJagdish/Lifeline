# Lifeline demo video script (under 3 minutes)

**Target duration:** 2:50 (170 seconds). **Recording status:** ready as a script; not recordable until the Lifeline scene compiles and the Preview journey passes. Do not use screenshots of a mockup as evidence of a working Lens. Record the actual Lens Studio Preview and show only behavior that works in that recording.

| Time | Shot and narration |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Show the actual Lifeline idle screen immediately. “Someone nearby collapses or is badly injured. In that moment, a bystander needs guidance while keeping their attention on the person and their hands free.” |
| 0:10–0:30 | Demonstrate manual pinch activation. Then show the separate Preview button labelled “SIMULATE VOICE: EMERGENCY.” Say clearly: “This button simulates the voice-trigger event; it does not demonstrate microphone recognition.” |
| 0:30–0:50 | Show the emergency home and 112 action. Demonstrate the call-instruction screen. “For this India-first version, the emergency number shown is 112. Lifeline does not place or verify a call; use a phone or ask someone nearby.” |
| 0:50–1:30 | Enter severe bleeding. Show one real instruction at a time. Demonstrate NEXT, BACK, and REPEAT. Keep each instruction legible on screen; do not scroll a long protocol. |
| 1:30–2:10 | Enter adult collapse. Show the adult-only boundary and the actual sourced compression wording/rate. State: “This is a visual guide only; it does not detect the patient or measure CPR quality. For children and infants, this guided pathway is out of scope; call 112 and follow the operator.” |
| 2:10–2:30 | Show the compact panel at normal Preview scale and its controls while the preview scene remains visible. Explain that the content is head-accessible and does not anchor to or recognize a patient. |
| 2:30–2:45 | Show the actual successful Preview journey and, only if scenarios have truly run, the LEAF result panel. Otherwise show the honest test report and say what remains unverified. |
| 2:45–2:50 | Closing: “Lifeline is a basic first-aid prompt companion for Indian bystanders. It supports emergency services; it does not replace them.” |

## Recording checks

- Keep the complete recording under 180 seconds, including title or end slate.
- Start with the working Lens, not an introduction or slide deck.
- Record real interactions and actual states in Lens Studio Preview. Do not splice an unimplemented path into a claimed live demo.
- Make the Preview simulation label visible and repeat the simulation limitation in narration.
- Never stage a real emergency, show a real patient, or imply clinical validation.
- If the Lens is still blocked, do not submit this as a working-Lens demo; record only after the checklist's Preview journey is verified.
