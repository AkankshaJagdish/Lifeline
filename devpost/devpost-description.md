# Devpost project description (draft)

**Lifeline** is a proposed hands-free first-aid companion for SPECS glasses, designed for ordinary bystanders in India who may be frightened and unsure what to do when someone nearby experiences severe bleeding or an adult collapse. It is designed around the Occupied Hands principle: keep 112 close at hand, present one concise, source-referenced instruction at a time, and let the bystander keep their eyes on the person instead of repeatedly looking down at a phone.

The planned MVP includes manual pinch activation, an optional supported “Emergency” ASR trigger, a clearly labelled Preview-only trigger simulation, an India-specific 112 emergency screen, short severe-bleeding and adult-only collapse paths, and a generic other-emergency fallback. Lens Studio and TypeScript provide the Lens runtime; SIK and UI Kit provide the intended spatial interaction patterns, and CLAD with Lens Studio MCP supports the authoring and testing workflow. Preview simulation is not microphone recognition, and Lifeline does not place or verify emergency calls.

**Current submission caveat:** The project source and planning documents are present, but the current scene is still the Spectacles Base Template, TypeScript compilation is blocked by package import errors, and no Lifeline Preview or LEAF test has passed. This description must not be submitted as a claim of a working MVP until the actual Lens compiles and the full Preview journey is demonstrated. Lifeline is not clinically validated or approved as a medical device, does not diagnose, and does not replace emergency services or operator instructions.
